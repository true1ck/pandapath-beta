About this app

PandaPay is a personal finance app for India. It builds your spending picture from the bank, card and wallet messages you already get, so you don't have to type in every payment.

What you can do

  • See where your money goes. Transactions from your bank and card SMS are sorted by category and merchant and shown by month.
  • Know which card earns more. Keep your credit cards in one place, see the rewards each one earns and the limits you have left, and check which card suits a purchase.
  • Split with friends. Add shared expenses, see who owes whom, and keep group and one-to-one balances in one list.
  • Stay on top of bills. Due-date reminders and a clear view of your card spends.
  • Fix what we get wrong. Edit any transaction, mark investments or transfers, and your corrections are kept.
  • Nearby-merchant alerts. Optional, and only if you turn on location.

Your messages stay on your phone

PandaPay reads bank SMS on your device, with your permission, and skips personal messages and one-time passwords. The message text is not sent to our servers. Only the transaction details (amount, merchant, date, card or account ending, and type) are synced, so your data shows on any phone you sign in to. You can turn SMS reading off in Settings at any time, and you can delete your account from Settings.

PandaPay does not lend money, move money or hold your funds. It is for people aged 18 and over.

This page counts visits and taps on the join buttons, by where the link came from. It uses no cookies and stores no IP address or other personal data.
